Net effect: Chinese frontier labs (DeepSeek, Alibaba Qwen, Moonshot Kimi, ByteDance Doubao) train primarily on grey‑market H100/H800 inventories, H20, and Huawei Ascend 910C (~600k shipments planned in 2026). The fence has not slowed the output: in July–August 2026 alone, Chinese labs shipped Kimi K3 (2.8T, largest open weights ever), Qwen3.8‑Max (2.4T, first open Max‑class flagship), DeepSeek V4‑Pro GA (1.6T, MIT), and GLM‑5.3 — and the fence itself is visibly shifting from chips to models (export orders, distillation accusations, cyber‑capability gates, watermarking mandates).

06

The picture, six paragraphs

Compute

From 3.1×10²³ FLOP (GPT‑3, Jun 2020) to ~5×10²⁶ FLOP (Grok 4, Jul 2025). Just over three orders of magnitude (~1,600×) in five years, doubling every five months. Cost per training run grew about 3.5× per year; chip price/performance grew about 1.4× per year. ~10 frontier models above 10²⁶ FLOP by start of 2026 (Epoch); the first frontier open‑weight model crossed 10²⁶ FLOP in late 2025.

Chips and dollars

NVIDIA datacenter revenue went from $2.98B in FY20 to $193.7B in FY26, growing 217% in FY24 alone. Big‑Five hyperscaler capex went from ~$160B in 2022 to a guided ~$680B in 2026. Capital intensity (capex / revenue) is now 45–57% at AWS / Meta / Microsoft / Google — historically these were 10–25% businesses. Free‑cash‑flow turned negative for several Big Five in 2026; the Big Five raised $108B of debt in 2025 alone.

Models

The 2024 reasoning shift (o1, Sep 2024) was the largest single‑architecture jump in benchmark history: +34.5 MATH, +26.7 GPQA, +65 AIME 2024 over GPT‑4o. By 2026 every frontier vendor ships hybrid (instant + thinking + pro) models, and mid‑2026 pushed to multi‑agent test‑time compute: GPT‑5.6 ultra (4–16 parallel agents), Claude Dynamic Workflows (hundreds of subagents), Kimi 300‑agent swarms. Open weights closed most of the gap — DeepSeek V4 Pro, Kimi K2.6/K2.7, Qwen 4, Llama 5, Mistral Voyage Pro — and in the May–June 2026 wave every major Western lab except OpenAI and Anthropic shipped downloadable weights, including xAI's first‑ever open drop (Grok 4 Open). Meanwhile Meta went dual‑track: its new closed frontier line (Muse Spark, from Meta Superintelligence Labs) now sits above the open Llama line, and with Muse Spark 1.1 (Jul 9) Meta started selling API access for the first time. Mid‑2026 also brought a new gate: Anthropic's Mythos‑class Fable 5 was suspended 18 days under a first‑of‑its‑kind US export‑control order, and GPT‑5.6 launched via a government‑coordinated limited preview. Then July–August went trillion‑parameter open: in four weeks Chinese labs shipped Kimi K3 (2.8T, the largest weights ever published), Qwen3.8‑Max (2.4T — the first open Max‑class flagship), DeepSeek V4‑Pro GA (1.6T), and GLM‑5.3, which now tops GDPval‑AA v2 outright. No Western lab shipped a new frontier base model in that window — Opus 5, Grok 4.6, and Muse Spark 1.2 were all post‑trains — because the next bases are all in the oven: OpenAI named Astra (Aug 1) alongside ten new machine‑checked mathematical results, then disclosed it may have hit the first‑ever "Critical" cyber designation (Aug 7); Gemini 4's "most ambitious pre‑training run yet" was confirmed Jul 21; Grok 5 (~6T) is training on Colossus 2. Consolidation continued: SpaceX closed its $60B Cursor acquisition (Aug 14), and the EU AI Act's watermarking regime took effect (Aug 2).

Benchmarks

The benchmarks the field invented to measure itself have all been consumed. MMLU (~92%), HumanEval (~98%), GSM8K (~99%), MATH (~99%), GPQA Diamond (94.9%, Grok 4.6 — top five within 0.7pp), MMMU original (~79%), AIME 2025 (~100% with tools), ARC‑AGI‑1 (87.5% at high compute) are saturated. ARC‑AGI‑2 (84.6%) and FrontierMath Tier 4 (~88% on the v2 set, up from ~19% in April) effectively fell in H1 2026, and Terminal‑Bench 2.1 now has six labs within a point of each other at 87–89% — so Terminal‑Bench 3.0 shipped in July and reset the board (top score 34.6%). The frontier now lives at SWE‑bench Pro (standardized), Humanity's Last Exam (with‑tools cluster 60–64.5%), OSWorld 2.0, SWE Marathon, Terminal‑Bench 3.0, and ARC‑AGI‑3 (top score: 7.8%).

Datacenters

2024: first 1‑GW campuses. 2026: first 2‑GW campuses operational (Colossus, Project Rainier). Stargate alone has committed >$450B of $500B for ~9 GW across seven US sites + UAE. US datacenter electricity went from 76 TWh (1.9% of US power) in 2018 to ~250 TWh (~7%) in 2025; LBNL projects 325–580 TWh by 2028. Datacenters drove ~50% of all US electricity demand growth in 2025.

Power as the binding constraint

Microsoft reports $80B in unfulfilled Azure orders tied to lack of electricity. Both Amazon's Indiana site and xAI's Memphis campus run heavily on on‑site gas turbines + Tesla Megapacks to bypass utility interconnect queues. Over 10 GW of new US nuclear has been contracted in 12 months — Three Mile Island restart, Kairos SMRs, X‑Energy, Susquehanna co‑location, Meta RFP. The chart, increasingly, is a chart of concrete and grid capacity.
