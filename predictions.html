Predictions to 2030

Eight high-confidence extrapolations based on trends visible in the factual report. We've kept this short on purpose — only projections where the trend is well-grounded in 3+ years of data, where the physical or economic constraints are legible, and where we can state clearly what would invalidate the call. The squinting, with our hands kept where you can see them.

How to read this page. Every claim has a confidence level (high / medium-high / medium), a "basis" pointing at the trend it sits on, and a "what would break this" clause. We deliberately don't make calls on AGI timing, bubble dynamics, China-US compute parity, or specific company outcomes — see §4 for why. Predictions will be revisited periodically; see the update log at the bottom.

01

Confidence framework

We don't pretend to know what 2030 looks like — but some sub-trends have been surprisingly steady for half a decade and are bumping into legible physical limits rather than algorithmic ones. Three confidence buckets:

High

Trend visible in 5+ years of data, multiple independent confirmations, near-term constraint already known. Order-of-magnitude error tolerated; direction is robust.

Medium-high

Trend is clear but the relevant ceiling is approaching (capital intensity, grid, fab capacity). Direction confident, magnitude wider.

Medium

Directionally confident based on observed dynamics, but a couple of plausible counter-trends could plausibly invert the call.

02

The compute trajectory

The chart we keep coming back to. Frontier FLOPs scaled ~5×/yr from GPT‑3 through Grok 4 (2020 → 2025), the most consistent compound trend in the data. Three projection bands: 3×/yr (slowdown), 4×/yr (Epoch's central case from 2024), and 5×/yr (continuation). All three converge on roughly 10²⁹ to 10³⁰ FLOP by 2030 — 1–2 OOM beyond Grok 4, ~6 OOM beyond GPT‑3.

Frontier training compute, 2020 → 2030 (projected)
Solid points: actual frontier runs (Epoch AI, vendor disclosures). Dashed lines: extrapolations from Grok 4 (Jul 2025).
Even the conservative 3×/yr line implies ~$50–100B per training run by 2030 at current cost trends — a level only reachable through cluster-sharing, multi-year training, or a sharp drop in $/FLOP. This is where the trend is most likely to bend.
03

The eight predictions

Ranked roughly by confidence. Each is paired with the trend it extrapolates and a clean break-clause: the specific observation in the next 1–2 years that would invalidate the call.

PREDICTION 01 High confidence

Frontier training compute reaches 10²⁹ – 10³⁰ FLOP by 2030

Conservative path (3×/yr): ~10²⁹ FLOP — 200× Grok 4. Central path (4×/yr, Epoch's recent estimate): ~5×10²⁹. Continuation (5×/yr): ~1.5×10³⁰. All three are within an order of magnitude of each other.

Basis

Five years of consistent ~5×/yr growth (Epoch AI). Grok 4 (5×10²⁶) and announced 2026‑27 runs on Stargate Abilene + Colossus 2 are already on this trend. Grid, capital, and chip supply are visible constraints, not surprises.

What would break this

A frontier lab failing to scale beyond ~10²⁷ for 18+ months despite available capital — i.e. a hard algorithmic or returns ceiling. We'd watch for whether the largest 2027 runs come in below 5×10²⁷.

PREDICTION 02 High confidence

US datacenter electricity hits 12–18% of the grid (~450–650 TWh)

Up from ~250 TWh (~7%) in 2025. The single most consequential physical buildout in the data. The bottleneck is interconnect queues and generation siting, not chip supply.

Basis

LBNL's 2024 study projects 325–580 TWh by 2028; we extend by 2 years. Microsoft, Google, AWS already have 5–10 GW of new capacity contracted but not energized. EIA shows queue depth doubled 2023→2025.

What would break this

A federal-level grid moratorium on hyperscale interconnect (politically possible, see Virginia 2025 fights), or a sustained crash in LLM unit economics that softens demand growth.

PREDICTION 03 High confidence

Multi-gigawatt AI campuses normalize: 5+ sites operating at >5 GW

Stargate (Abilene + sister sites, ~5 GW total announced), Colossus 2 (xAI Memphis, 2 GW operating, 5 GW capacity target), Project Rainier (AWS, 2.2 GW), Hyperion (Meta), plus at least one Anthropic campus and 1–2 Google equivalents. Largest single campus by 2030: ~10 GW.

Basis

Five of these are already permitted or under construction in early 2026. Largest single campus: 1 GW (2024) → 2 GW (2026) → 5 GW announced for 2027. Linear extrapolation in the chart on the factual report. Concrete is poured; this is the most physically locked-in projection.

What would break this

Cancellation of two or more announced 5 GW phases, which would require a discrete shock (model demand collapse, capex retrenchment by 2 of MSFT/GOOG/META/AMZN simultaneously).

PREDICTION 04 Medium-high

Big-Five capex growth bends from 43%/yr to ~15–20%/yr; cumulative annual hits ~$1.2–1.8T by 2030

Capex grew 4.25× from 2022 ($160B) to 2026 ($680B). Continuing that pace would put 2030 at $4T+ — economically incoherent. We expect a sharper bend than has yet been visible in guidance.

Basis

Capital intensity (capex/revenue) is at 45–57% across MSFT/GOOG/META/AMZN — historic high; these were 10–25% businesses pre‑2022. Free cash flow already turned negative for several Big Five in 2025‑26. $108B of debt raised in 2025 alone. The capacity to keep growing capex at 40%+/yr against falling FCF runs out by ~2027‑28.

What would break this

A successful pivot to inference-revenue-funded buildouts (i.e. AI revenue scales fast enough that capex/revenue ratios normalize without slowing capex). Watch capital intensity in 2027 quarterlies — if it's still >45% in 2H'27, growth has more room.

PREDICTION 05 High confidence

The current benchmark suite is fully saturated by 2027–2028; new benchmarks shift to long-horizon agentic tasks

Saturation: Humanity's Last Exam, FrontierMath Tier 4, ARC-AGI-2, SWE-bench Pro all crossed by frontier closed models. What replaces them: month-long autonomous software tasks, reproducible scientific findings, multi-agent orchestration benchmarks. The old "single-question score" format will look quaint by 2028.

Basis

Time-to-saturation trend is documented in the factual report's §3 table: 2020 benchmarks took 3–4 yr, 2023 cohort took 2–3 yr, 2024 cohort solved within ~12 months. HLE already at 53% (Opus 4.6 with tools, Feb 2026). FrontierMath has Tier 4 because Tiers 1‑3 fell. The pattern repeats.

What would break this

A deliberately-designed benchmark that resists 18 months of effort from frontier labs (METR's RE-Bench, OpenAI's Preparedness evals are candidates). One has now lasted >18 mo and we'd update.

PREDICTION 06 Medium-high

5–15 GW of new nuclear and SMR capacity comes online dedicated to AI

Lower bound is essentially locked. Three Mile Island restart (835 MW, Microsoft, target 2027‑28), Susquehanna (Amazon, 960 MW, operational), Kairos SMRs (Google, ~500 MW by 2030), X-Energy SMRs (Amazon, 5 GW by 2039 with first plants 2030). Upper bound depends on whether SMRs can actually deploy at scale or slip to 2032+.

Basis

10+ GW already contracted in 12 months ending Q1 2026 — the largest single‑sector nuclear procurement in 50 years. NRC has approved 2 SMR designs, 4 more under review. RFPs from Meta and Oracle still open. Hyperscaler willingness-to-pay is at $100/MWh+ levels that re-open mothballed plants.

What would break this

SMR vendors slipping first-of-a-kind operational dates past 2030 (Kairos, X‑Energy both have 2027‑28 targets that history suggests will slip). Lower bound holds even if all SMRs miss; upper requires SMRs to deliver.

PREDICTION 07 Medium-high

Custom silicon takes 30–50% of new accelerator spending

Up from ~15–20% in 2025‑26. Google TPU v8/v9, AWS Trainium 3, Microsoft Maia 200, Meta MTIA-Next all in serious volume by 2027. NVIDIA's near-monopoly on training ends; their inference share holds longer. Total NVIDIA datacenter revenue still grows in absolute terms — just slower than the market.

Basis

Each hyperscaler has now shipped 3+ generations of internal silicon. TSMC 2 nm allocation reportedly split 60/40 NVIDIA/hyperscalers for 2027 vs 80/20 today. Custom silicon $/training‑FLOP advantage is 30–50% on first-party workloads. Trainium 3 launched on cadence; Maia 200 announced for late 2026.

What would break this

NVIDIA delivering an order-of-magnitude generational leap (Rubin Ultra → "Feynman" tier) that resets the price/perf gap. Plausible but not predicted by current roadmap.

PREDICTION 08 Medium

Open-weight frontier persists at a 6–12 month lag behind closed

Closed crossed 10²⁶ FLOP in 2024; open-weight crossed in late 2025 (DeepSeek V3 → V4). The gap has been compressing. By 2030, expect the strongest open-weight model to be 10²⁸–10²⁹ FLOP, behind frontier closed by 6–12 months on benchmarks.

Basis

DeepSeek, Alibaba (Qwen), Moonshot (Kimi), Mistral, Meta have all sustained release cadences. Cost-per-FLOP advantage on Chinese clusters (Huawei 910C, MI325X) means 10²⁷ open is feasible at <$200M training cost by 2027. Alignment of incentives — non-US labs, infrastructure providers — keeps open weights flowing.

What would break this

(a) A Chinese frontier lab pulling weights private to compete with closed labs commercially; (b) US export controls successfully constraining 2 nm fabrication for non‑NVIDIA buyers; (c) Meta abandoning Llama. Any single one of these would widen the gap; all three combined would close the open frontier.

04

What we're not predicting (and why)

A list of common 2030-AI claims we deliberately leave out. Either the trend isn't legible enough, or the prediction depends on definitions that aren't settled, or the variance swamps the signal. Update, July 2026: several of these (bubble dynamics, China, company-layer outcomes) are now addressed with explicit scenario weights — a different, lower-confidence tool — on the economics scenarios page.

AGI / "transformative AI" timing
No consensus definition; pundit calls range from 2027 to 2045+. Forecasting AGI is forecasting a target that moves with each capability jump. We'll track capabilities, not labels.
Whether the bubble pops
Binary outcome with low signal. Capital-intensity stress (Pred. 04) is the trend we can read; the timing of any reset is dominated by macro factors outside this dataset.
China-US compute parity
Depends on Huawei Ascend production scaling (currently ~600k 910C/yr planned 2026) vs. US export-control enforcement vs. SMIC 5 nm yields. Three independent variables, each with wide ranges; product is wider still.
Specific company outcomes
Industry has reformed twice already (closed‑lab specialization, vertical integration). Calling specific winners requires reading product-market fit, which 2030 will surface, not us.
Algorithmic breakthroughs
By definition unforecastable. The rise of test‑time compute (Sep 2024) and the o1 paradigm took the field by surprise. Next paradigm could emerge any quarter.
AI economic impact in $ terms
McKinsey/Goldman estimates range from $2T to $20T+ added GDP by 2030. That spread is the prediction. Watch revenue at the lab level instead — concrete and disclosed.
Geopolitical shocks (Taiwan, etc.)
A Taiwan disruption alone resets all chip-related predictions to "unknown." We're conditioning on baseline geopolitics; if that breaks, this whole page is moot.
